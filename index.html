NETW 112

Containers & Orchestration

Fifteen weeks from your first podman run to operating production applications on Red Hat OpenShift. This site is the course text and practice space; your graded labs happen in the Red Hat Online Learning Environment.

The semester

Two halves, each ending in a hands-on practical. Weeks you can open now are outlined; the rest are being written. Your progress is saved in this browser.

Part 1: Containers with Podman

Weeks 1 to 9, alongside DO188, toward EX188

    Part 2: Operating OpenShift

    Weeks 10 to 15, alongside DO180, toward EX280

      How each week works

      Every week follows the same rhythm, matching the instructor-demo then hands-on-lab pattern of class sessions.

      1. Read here

        Short sections explain the concepts and commands, with the reasoning behind them.

      2. Practice here

        Simulators, explorers and an ungraded knowledge check let you try ideas safely and as often as you like.

      3. Lab in the OLE

        Each week's Lab bridge points you to the matching Red Hat guided exercises and graded lab.

      Optional enrichment

      Try containers on your own computer

      Install Docker or Podman on your own Windows or Mac laptop, then use containers for real work: spin up a database in seconds, try a language without installing it, build a reproducible dev environment, or set up a disposable sandbox and vulnerable targets for security practice. Especially useful for computer science and cybersecurity students.

      What you'll be able to do

      There are no written exams. Each outcome is demonstrated by performing real tasks in a live lab environment.

      Course learning outcomes and where they are measured
      OutcomeTaughtMeasured
      1. Run, manage and troubleshoot containers on RHEL using Podman and tools such as Skopeo.Weeks 1 to 3, 6Midterm practical
      2. Build custom images with Containerfiles and manage them through public and private registries.Weeks 3 and 4Midterm practical
      3. Deploy multi-container apps with Podman Compose, including persistent storage configured correctly for SELinux.Weeks 5 and 7Midterm practical
      4. Deploy, configure and manage applications on OpenShift with the web console and the oc CLI, including networking, configuration data, secrets and storage.Weeks 8, 10 to 14Final practicals
      5. Keep applications reliable with health probes, resource limits, autoscaling and controlled updates with rollback.Weeks 14 and 15Final practicals

      How your grade is built

      Labs 50%
      Midterm 20%
      Finals 30%

      Weekly OLE labs are graded on completion and correctness. Labs up to one week late earn up to 80% credit, later than that up to 50%. Practical deadlines are firm. See the syllabus for the full policy.
