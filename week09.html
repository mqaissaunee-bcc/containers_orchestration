1About the practical

The midterm lab practical measures Outcomes 1 to 3: running and troubleshooting containers, building images and using registries, and deploying a multi-container application with correctly configured storage. As with every graded assessment in this course, there is no written exam. You'll perform real tasks in a live lab environment, and the result is checked.

What to expect, from the syllabus:

  • It's the Chapter 9 comprehensive review lab in the OLE, and it's worth 20% of your grade.
  • The deadline is firm. Exceptions need written approval from your instructor before the due date.
  • It's individual work. AI tools may not be used to complete the tasks. Documentation, man pages and --help are fair game unless your instructor says otherwise.
  • Report lab environment problems immediately; they can't be accepted as a reason for lateness after the fact.

3Practical strategy

  1. Read everything first. Skim every task before typing. Later tasks often depend on names, ports or files from earlier ones, and you'll spot those links.
  2. Budget your time. Divide the time by the number of tasks. If one task eats twice its share, move on and come back.
  3. Copy names exactly. Container names, image tags, ports, volume names and paths are checked literally. A typo in practise-db fails a task you otherwise did perfectly.
  4. Verify every task as you finish it.podman ps, curl, podman logs, podman inspect --format. Don't assume; prove.
  5. Use the method when stuck. State, logs, configuration, inside versus outside, one change at a time.
  6. Leave no half-finished state. A stopped container with the right name may score worse than a running one with a small mistake. Check the final state of everything before you finish.
  7. Know your built-in help.podman run --help, man podman-run, man containers-registries.conf, podman-compose --help.
The three most common lost points

A bind mount without :Z. A database started without its required environment variables. A container that's running but not reachable because the container port in -p is wrong. Check all three before you call a task done.

4Command recall
